Delivery

We deliver in Accra through same-day and next-day couriers, and nationwide through Ghana Post, Flash Express and DHL eCommerce. Delivery is confirmed with a tracking number once your order is handed to the partner.

Returns & refunds

You have 7 days from delivery to return an item. It must be unworn, unwashed and have its original tags. Return shipping is on you unless the item is defective or was mis-described. Refunds are processed within 3-5 business days after we inspect the return.

Size help

Thrift sizing varies between labels. Each listing includes the labelled size and a description of the fit. If you are between sizes, message us with your bust, waist and hip measurements in centimetres and we will help you choose.
